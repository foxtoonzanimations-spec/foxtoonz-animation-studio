01 / ABOUT

THE STUDIO

Stories with a
distinctly African voice.

FoxToonz Animation Studio is a creative arts organisation specialising in animation, digital arts and related creative activities. We bring artists and storytellers together to create quality work rooted in imagination, culture and collaboration.

Our goal is to develop animation talent in Zambia, create opportunities for creatives and present African stories through film, series, digital content and creative services.
